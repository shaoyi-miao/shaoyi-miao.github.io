Opportunity · Time · Open discussion

Why can every requirement be met, yet nothing gets done?

When there is money, there is no time; when there is time, the opportunity has passed. The problem may be more than a shortage of any single resource.

My current view

I tend to focus on whether the necessary conditions can support action simultaneously within the same effective window of time, rather than simply checking whether each condition has been met at some point. An opportunity, the ability to act and other people's availability sometimes need to overlap.

Conditions can also be secured in advance through saving, delegation, reservations, learning or arrangements that can be deferred. Some conditions that appear to require simultaneous fulfilment can actually be met separately.

What remains unclear

I still want to understand which real-world tasks require a shared time window, and which can overcome timing mismatches through institutions, technology or advance accumulation. Examples alone cannot establish how important this mechanism is relative to an overall shortage of resources.

What would change my mind

If actions with separately satisfied conditions can still be completed successfully by preserving those conditions across time, the judgment about necessary overlap needs revision. Comparisons of specific successes and failures are welcome, especially where they identify which conditions can be preserved and which expire.
