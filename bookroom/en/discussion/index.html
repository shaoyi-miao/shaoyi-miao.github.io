OPEN QUESTIONS

Questions I have not settled

There are questions on which I have a tentative view but no sufficiently convincing answer. I am sharing them here so that different experiences, explanations and evidence can change my thinking.

Discussion

Open to counterexamples
Relationships · Freedom · Open discussionAre people more willing to stay when they are free to leave?Is commitment in a relationship stable because leaving is difficult, or more meaningful because leaving is possible?↗Personal change · Open discussionWhy can understanding yourself leave your life unchanged?Is explaining a predicament part of getting out of it, or another way of staying where you are?↗Habits · Ability · Open discussionWhen tasks become easier, can people lose the ability to do them independently?Tools, automation and outsourcing reduce everyday costs. But where does the effort they save actually go?↗Human nature · Ethics · Open discussionHow much can a person's principles cost before they give way?When someone's behaviour changes under pressure, how can I distinguish a change in values from a change in practical circumstances?↗Opportunity · Time · Open discussionWhy can every requirement be met, yet nothing gets done?When there is money, there is no time; when there is time, the opportunity has passed. The problem may be more than a shortage of any single resource.↗

These are not established conclusions. If my view changes, I will update the question and the reasons, while preserving the discussion.
